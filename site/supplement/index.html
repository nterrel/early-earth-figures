Energy evolution

Temperature and potential-energy counts in the 22.8-million-atom run

The full heating/hot/cooling view retains all 366,067 reported records. Both panels use 400×400 square cells; the dotted box marks the enlarged 2500 K view. Colors show records per bin on a logarithmic scale.

Figure details

Full bins: 6.275 K × 432,500 kcal/mol; zoom: 0.025 K × 12,500 kcal/mol. Zoom bounds: 2495–2505 K and −5.2590×10¹⁰ to −5.2585×10¹⁰ kcal/mol. Of 334,062 constant-2500 K records, 331,366 are visible and 2,696 outside the zoom. Temperature SD 0.435 K. Raw total potential energy in kcal/mol, without reference subtraction or per-atom normalization. Repeated/restarted records retained and correlated. Author-approved rendering; final manuscript selection remains open. Reference-figure normalization and cross-size comparison remain unresolved. Alanine improper-torsion distribution
