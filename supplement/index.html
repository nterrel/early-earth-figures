Energy evolution

Temperature and potential-energy counts in the 22.8-million-atom run

The full heating/hot/cooling histogram retains all 366,067 reported records. An enlarged 2500 K panel uses 0.1 K bins to resolve the narrow temperature spread; colors show counts on a logarithmic scale without a saturation cap.

Figure details

Full bins: 10 K × 500,000 kcal mol−1; close-up: 0.1 K × 50,000 kcal mol−1. Constant-2500 K stages selected from echoed thermostat commands because restart step counters reset: 334,062 records, temperature SD 0.435 K. Potential energy is the raw total minus the initial 300 K median (−52,699,686,000 kcal mol−1), displayed in millions; no per-atom or unresolved historical-axis normalization. Repeated/restarted records retained. No measured 228-atom fluctuation comparison, equilibrium claim or product assignment. Receipt: analysis/energy_hysteresis/results/reconstruction.json.

Alanine improper-torsion distribution

The N–Cα–C(carboxyl)–Cβ improper-torsion histogram for 438,986 alanine-reference coordinate blocks has two modes at ±124.2°, describing the orientation of substituents around the central carbon.

Figure details
