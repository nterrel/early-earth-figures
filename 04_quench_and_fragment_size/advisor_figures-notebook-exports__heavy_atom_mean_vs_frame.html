How does counting hydrogen-only fragments affect mean size? — F19¶

Point: hot and quenched sizes are much closer when hydrogen-only fragments are excluded. Both figures use the same axes; blue = hot, orange = quenched.

  • Including H-only: C+N+O atoms / all original fragments; H-only fragments contribute zero.
  • Excluding H-only: C+N+O atoms / fragments containing at least one non-H atom.
